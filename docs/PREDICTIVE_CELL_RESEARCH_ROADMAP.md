## Separate aging research specification (deferred execution)

Scientific tasks: predict held-out senescence labels/transcriptional age signatures; compare against unchanged/mean/linear baselines; optionally estimate epigenetic age only with suitable methylation inputs. Proteostasis, mitochondrial stress and DNA-damage readouts remain separate endpoints. Require published labels, cell-line/tissue identity, donor, passage, exposure, batch and longitudinal identifiers; separate donors and datasets before fitting. No study selected, data downloaded, model trained or aging benchmark executed in this stage.

Suggested metrics: held-out MAE/calibration for continuous signatures; AUROC/AUPRC and prevalence-aware simple baselines for prespecified categorical labels; time-stratified error for actual longitudinal measurements. Replicate donor/batch uncertainty, not cells as independent donors. Age-marker correlations do not prove mechanisms, lifespan prediction or reversal. A dedicated aging research branch should begin with a licensed reproducible dataset and this protocol, independently of the current predictive integration branch.
